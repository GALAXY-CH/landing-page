Privacy Policy

Galaxy Switzerland (usegalaxy.ch) · Effective date: 1 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains which personal data the Galaxy Switzerland service collects, why, where it is stored, and what rights you have. We process personal data in accordance with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where it applies to you, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Who is responsible

Galaxy Switzerland is a national platform for open, reproducible science, funded by swissuniversities and led by ETH Zurich. The controller responsible for processing your personal data is:

ETH Zurich (Eidgenössische Technische Hochschule Zürich)
Rämistrasse 101
8092 Zurich, Switzerland
Email: support@usegalaxy.ch

Data Protection Officer of ETH Zurich: ds@ethz.ch, or by post to ETH Zurich, Legal Office (Data Protection), Rämistrasse 101, 8092 Zurich, Switzerland. See also the ETH Zurich data protection statement.

2. Scope

This policy covers the Galaxy service at usegalaxy.ch and its sign-in service at auth.usegalaxy.ch (together, the “Service”). It does not cover external websites that we link to, which have their own privacy policies.

3. Data we process

3.1 Account data

You sign in with Switch edu-ID or MyAccessID (EOSC). Your identity provider sends us the attributes needed to create and identify your account, typically a unique identifier, your name and your email address. Galaxy stores your email address and a public username. We use this data to provide your account, to prevent abuse of the Service, and to send you service notices.

You can also use Galaxy without signing in. Your work is then linked to a session cookie only, and is lost when that cookie is deleted.

3.2 Data you upload or create

We store the datasets you upload, the histories, workflows and results you create, and the metadata of the jobs you run (for example the tool, its parameters, run times and resources used). We process this data only to run your analyses and to operate the Service. Data you choose to share or publish becomes visible to others as described in our Terms of Service.

3.3 Technical and log data

When you use the Service, our web servers process technical data such as your IP address, the pages and API endpoints requested, the time of the request and your browser type. We use it to deliver the Service, to keep it secure and to troubleshoot problems. Server logs, including IP addresses, are kept in rotating log files and are normally overwritten within about a week. They are used only for operating and securing the Service and are not combined with your account data. We also collect operational metrics (for example job counts and system load), mostly in aggregated form, and keep them for 30 days.

3.4 LLM Hub

If you use the AI (large language model) tools, the text and files you send to a model are processed by the inference service of the Swiss National Supercomputing Centre (CSCS) in Switzerland. We record, per account, how many requests and tokens you use, in order to apply fair-use limits. We do not store the content of your prompts outside your Galaxy history.

3.5 Emails and support requests

We send service emails (for example account and job notifications) to your registered address. If you contact support@usegalaxy.ch, we use your message and contact details only to answer your request. The support address is an email alias on Google Workspace that forwards your message to the support team, and our service emails are sent through Google Workspace's mail relay (see section 5).

4. Cookies and tracking

Galaxy and the sign-in service use cookies that are strictly necessary to keep you signed in and to keep your session. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not use third-party analytics services.

Some images on the Galaxy welcome page are loaded from GitHub. When your browser loads them, GitHub receives your IP address.

5. Where your data is stored and who processes it

Your data and analyses stay in Switzerland. Only emails (Google) and the website and welcome-page images (GitHub) involve providers outside Switzerland. The Service runs on the infrastructure listed below. These providers process data only on our behalf and for the purposes described here.

Provider Purpose Location
SWITCH Cloud servers and storage running Galaxy; storage of your data and of database backups Switzerland
CSCS (Swiss National Supercomputing Centre) Larger analyses on the Alps supercomputer (your input and output data is placed on its scratch storage while a job runs); LLM Hub inference Switzerland
Switch edu-ID; MyAccessID (GÉANT) Sign-in (identity providers you choose to log in with) Switzerland; EU
Google (Google Workspace) Email only: forwarding of messages sent to support@usegalaxy.ch to the support team, and relay of outgoing service emails May process data outside Switzerland, including in the United States
GitHub Hosting of this website and of images on the Galaxy welcome page United States

We do not sell your personal data or share it with third parties for their own purposes, unless we are required to do so by law.

6. Sensitive data

Do not upload unencrypted human or patient data, or any other sensitive personal data (such as health or genetic data that can identify a person). Galaxy Switzerland is a shared research platform. It is not designed or certified for clinical or other sensitive personal data, and Galaxy does not encrypt stored data at the application level.

If your data is subject to restrictions, please check with your institution, ethics committee or project lead before you upload it.

7. How long we keep data

  • Account data and your Galaxy data: for as long as your account is in use, or until you delete the data. Datasets and histories you delete are permanently removed after about 7 days.
  • Inactive accounts: if you do not log in for 12 months, we notify you. If you do not log in within one month of that notice, your account is deactivated, and your data is deleted six months later. We keep an account record (username, email address and analysis metadata) that can be reactivated on request; it is deleted after 10 years without activity. Details are in our Terms of Service.
  • Work done without an account: deleted automatically once it has not been changed for 7 days, and permanently removed within about three weeks.
  • Database backups: 30 days.
  • Server logs: normally overwritten within about a week (see 3.3).
  • Operational metrics: 30 days.
  • LLM Hub usage records: for as long as your account exists.

8. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you. Your account details are shown in Galaxy under User → Preferences → Manage Information; for anything else, contact us.
  • Correct inaccurate data, under Manage Information or by contacting us.
  • Delete your data and account. You can delete datasets and histories yourself at any time. To delete your account, email support@usegalaxy.ch.
  • Take your data with you by downloading your datasets or exporting your histories and workflows from Galaxy.
  • Object to processing, or withdraw your consent, with effect for the future.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) or, if the GDPR applies to you, with the supervisory authority of your EU country.

We do not use your data for profiling or for decisions based solely on automated processing.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the Service changes. The current version and its effective date are always published on this page. We will announce significant changes on usegalaxy.ch.

10. Contact

For questions about this policy or to exercise your rights, contact support@usegalaxy.ch. You can also contact our data protection officer at ds@ethz.ch.
